# SEO boundaries: Treasure Valley Mental Health Guide vs. Bright Hope Therapy

## Goal
Use the two sites to create two complementary discovery paths without having them chase the same search intent.

## Bright Hope Therapy owns practice / transactional intent
Bright Hope Therapy should remain the primary target for searches where the user is looking for Lisa Bright or for a specific therapy service from a practice in Meridian.

Examples of Bright Hope-owned intent:
- therapist / counselor in Meridian
- EMDR therapy in Meridian
- trauma / PTSD therapy in Meridian
- anxiety / stress therapy in Meridian
- grief, depression, chronic pain, life transitions, relationship concerns, self-esteem and related service pages
- Lisa Bright / Bright Hope Therapy branded searches
- appointment / consultation / practice-specific conversion searches

Bright Hope should continue using distinct service pages and natural Treasure Valley service-area mentions. Do not create thin Boise/Eagle/Nampa/Kuna/Star doorway pages solely to duplicate the same service copy by city.

## Treasure Valley Mental Health Guide owns directory / comparison / informational intent
TVMHG should target searches where the user wants to discover, compare, understand, or narrow local mental-health options.

Examples of TVMHG-owned intent:
- find a counselor in the Treasure Valley
- counselors accepting a specific insurance carrier or plan
- counselors by specialty, treatment approach, visit type, availability, population, or distance
- how to choose a counselor
- informational mental-health topics and treatment-option explainers
- regional provider directory searches across Meridian, Boise, Eagle, Nampa, Kuna, Star, Caldwell, and nearby Treasure Valley communities

## Content rules
1. Do not create TVMHG commercial service pages that mirror Bright Hope URLs or intent, such as an "EMDR therapy Meridian Idaho" landing page designed to convert directly to treatment.
2. TVMHG topic pages should be educational and directory-oriented: explain the topic or treatment, then help the visitor find relevant providers.
3. Bright Hope service pages should remain practice-oriented: Lisa's approach, who the service is for, what to expect, and the practice's appointment CTA.
4. Provider profiles on TVMHG should use concise directory summaries rather than copying a provider's practice website page word-for-word.
5. TVMHG directory sorting and filtering must remain neutral. Bright Hope receives no ranking preference because of ownership or sponsorship.
6. Filter/query-string states should not become separate thin SEO landing pages by default. The canonical directory remains `/find-counselor` unless a future landing page has substantial unique regional content and a clear non-duplicative purpose.
7. Provider profile pages may be indexable when published, but should point visitors outward to the provider's own website when they want practice-specific information or scheduling.

## Insurance search strategy
Insurance is a directory/comparison feature and therefore belongs strongly on TVMHG rather than Bright Hope SEO pages.

- Providers select broad carriers/payment options.
- Providers may add exact contracted plan/network names using the format `Carrier — Plan`.
- The public directory displays specific plan filters only when at least one published provider reports that plan.
- Search results should not imply guaranteed coverage. Display a reminder that network participation and benefits can change and should be confirmed with the clinician and insurer.
- Advanced analytics may aggregate insurance-filter usage subject to the existing privacy threshold; do not expose individual visitor identities or raw sensitive searches.

## Measurement
Track the two properties separately in Search Console and analytics. Watch:
- Bright Hope organic impressions/clicks for practice and service queries
- TVMHG impressions/clicks for directory/comparison queries
- TVMHG provider-profile views
- outbound clicks from Lisa's TVMHG profile to Bright Hope
- contact/scheduling clicks
- query overlap between the two domains

If both domains begin ranking for the same query family, first compare search intent before changing content. The preferred outcome is not "only one domain ranks"; it is that each domain ranks for the intent it is best suited to satisfy.
